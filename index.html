Königsforst

Königsforst 51107, Köln

Plan a Saturday in the forest

A young mother wants to spend a Saturday afternoon exploring the Königsforst with her 10-year-old daughter and their dog. They are looking for an easy, family-friendly walking route with interesting things to discover along the way. Before setting off, the mother searches online for suitable trails, a convenient starting point and information about the rules for dogs. However, the information is spread across several websites, making it difficult to find everything she needs at a glance. A central, mobile-friendly website could bring together walking routes, directions, family-friendly highlights and dog regulations, helping them plan a fun and stress-free day outdoors.

What would you like to know?

  1. Where can I enter the forest, and which walking and cycling routes are available?
  2. What can I discover in the forest? Flora and fauna?
  3. How do I get there by car, train or bike?
  4. What rules do I need to follow? Are there guided walks, tours or upcoming events?
  5. Are the paths suitable for people with limited mobility? What should I know before visiting today, such as hunting times?
Enjoy your adventure, explore gently and have a great time on your visit.
